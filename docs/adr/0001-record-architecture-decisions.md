# 1. Record architecture decisions

Date: 2026-11-08 · Status: accepted

## Context

EinKaufZettel is a side project that will be paused and resumed. Reasons behind decisions get lost quickly.

## Decision

Significant decisions are recorded as short Architecture Decision Records in `docs/adr`, numbered in order.
Each record states context, decision and consequences. Superseded records stay and link to their successor.

## Consequences

A decision that is not written down here is open for discussion.
