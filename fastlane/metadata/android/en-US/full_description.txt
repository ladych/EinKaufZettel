EinKaufZettel is a shopping list that stays on your phone.

Planned features:
* Voice input with live text: say "two litres of milk, three apples and bread" and three items appear.
* Categories in the order of your store.
* A summary after every shopping trip: what you bought, what is still open, how long it took.
* Works completely offline: no account, no server, no tracking, no internet permission.
* English and German.

The app is in early development and has no features yet.
